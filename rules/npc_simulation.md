# NPC Simulation Rules

These systems govern how alive the world feels.

## Romance System
Mode: B (Dynamic Realistic)

- Romance can naturally diverge from canon.
- Attraction, chemistry, compatibility, trust, emotional bonding, timing, maturity, and shared experiences matter.
- Romance should feel realistic and earned.
- No forced pairings.
- Canon relationships are starting tendencies, not guarantees.

## Friendship System
Mode: B (Dynamic)

Characters remember:

- loyalty
- support
- betrayal
- shared hardship
- training together
- respect
- reliability

Friendships can strengthen or weaken dynamically.

## Rivalry System
Mode: B (Dynamic)

Rivalries may evolve beyond canon.

Possible examples:

- healthy rivalry
- bitter rivalry
- mutual respect rivalry
- mentor rivalry

Canon rivalries are tendencies, not hard locks.

## Mentorship System
Mode: C (Extremely Simulated)

Mentorship matters heavily.

Mentors influence:

- combat style
- philosophy
- discipline
- emotional development
- ki control
- technique learning
- training efficiency
- personality growth

Different mentors create meaningfully different growth paths.

Examples:

Master Roshi:
- martial fundamentals
- discipline
- survival mindset
- unconventional training

Kami:
- calmness
- awareness
- ki control
- wisdom

King Kai:
- advanced techniques
- strange training methods
- efficiency

## Reputation System
Mode: B (Dynamic)

The world reacts to:

- kindness
- recklessness
- violence
- heroism
- selfishness
- honor
- reliability

Reputation affects dialogue, trust, opportunities, and reactions.

## Party / World Autonomy
Mode: C (Full AI Simulation)

Characters act independently.

NPCs may:

- train on their own
- make plans
- change opinions
- improve skills
- build relationships
- argue
- drift apart
- make mistakes
- become stronger or weaker

The world should feel alive even when Goku is not present.

Characters should not wait frozen for the player.
