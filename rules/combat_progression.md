# Combat & Progression Philosophy

## Combat Realism
Mode: B (Mixed)

Combat should balance anime spectacle with logical simulation.

Important factors:

- stamina
- injuries
- fear
- confidence
- terrain
- combat intelligence
- technique compatibility
- power level differences
- exhaustion

Fights should feel dramatic but internally logical.

## Player Choice During Fights

Battles should usually be resolved in short combat beats with player choices between beats.

Default flow:

1. Opening clash or threat reveal.
2. Brief position/status update.
3. Player choices for Goku's next action.
4. Continue until the fight is logically decided.

Exceptions:

- the power gap makes the result an obvious one-shot
- the player chooses a clear finishing action
- the scene is not a meaningful fight
- Goku loses consciousness or control, in which case the narration follows Goku's perspective and skips to when he returns to awareness

## Training Pace
Mode: B (Balanced)

Progression should feel satisfying.

- noticeable growth over time
- earned improvement
- no absurd grind
- no instant overpowered jumps

Growth should feel rewarding without becoming tedious.

## Saiyan Near-Death Growth
Mode: B (Semi-Systemized)

Zenkai boosts exist.

Rules:

- must be meaningful survival situations
- not every injury triggers growth
- repeated abuse becomes less effective
- biology and circumstances matter

Growth should feel exciting, not exploitable.

## Technique Creativity
Mode: B (Flexible)

Creative technique use is encouraged.

Examples:

- unusual Kamehameha uses
- terrain interaction
- trick shots
- mobility use
- feints
- tactical experimentation

Creativity should be rewarded when logical.

## Villain Intelligence
Mode: B (Smarter)

Villains should act intelligently.

Enemies:

- learn patterns
- adapt somewhat
- use advantages
- exploit weaknesses

But should still remain faithful to personality.

Example:

- Vegeta may be arrogant.
- Frieza may toy with enemies.
- Cell may adapt rapidly.

## Hidden Power Spikes
Mode: B (Dynamic)

Emotion matters.

Possible temporary influences:

- rage
- fear
- instinct
- desperation
- responsibility
- confidence

Power spikes should feel meaningful and emotional rather than random.
