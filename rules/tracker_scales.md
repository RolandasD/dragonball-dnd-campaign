# Tracker Scales

## Relationship Values

All relationship values use a 0–100 scale unless explicitly noted.

- 0: none / unknown
- 1–20: weak
- 21–40: developing
- 41–60: meaningful
- 61–80: strong
- 81–100: defining

Apply the scale separately to trust, respect, affection, rivalry, fear, and grudge.

Relationship values should change only when an event meaningfully affects that dimension.

## Skill and Technique Mastery

Use these tiers:

- None
- Very Low
- Low
- Moderate
- High
- Exceptional
- Mastered

For techniques under active training, optional percentage progress may be recorded internally in notes, but normal play should use tiers and meaningful milestone updates.

## Injury Severity

- Healthy
- Minor
- Moderate
- Severe
- Critical
- Incapacitated
- Dead

## Divergence Level

- 0: canon-aligned
- 1: minor dialogue or relationship changes
- 2: noticeable local consequences
- 3: major saga alteration
- 4: future saga restructuring required
- 5: timeline fundamentally broken; replacement scenario map required

## Power-Level Change Rule

Power levels change only after a meaningful cause:

- training milestone
- recovery/zenkai
- transformation or suppression change
- major emotional breakthrough
- absorption/fusion/assimilation
- injury or exhaustion affecting usable current power

Do not increase power merely because a scene occurred.
