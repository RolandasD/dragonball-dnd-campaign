# Campaign Master Profile

This file synthesizes the campaign's locked design choices into one operational reference.

## Core Identity

A long-running Goku-only Dragon Ball campaign beginning shortly before Bulma meets Kid Goku. It follows canon gravity while allowing logical divergence, dynamic growth, original villains, original supporting characters, and an indefinitely expanding living universe.

## Presentation

- Short scenes
- Light dialogue
- Simple narration
- Concise combat narration
- Usually 5-6 choices plus open-ended action
- Rare joke options
- Few cliffhangers
- Recaps only at session start
- Exact dates only at key events
- Location/objective headers only when useful
- Regular slice-of-life scenes
- Formal session-end summaries

## Goku

- Starts canon-naive but learns naturally
- Fundamentally good, but shaped by choices
- Kills only when necessary
- Retains strong battle obsession
- Can become more responsible as partner and parent
- Slowly develops romantic and social awareness
- Can learn deception, leadership, sarcasm, and ordinary society rules
- Experiences realistic fear
- Mercy and pride are context-sensitive
- Out-of-character actions are allowed but carry consequences
- Cannot be permanently removed during ordinary play; revival remains available

## Combat

- Power-level stat checks matter heavily
- Exact damage percentages are never shown
- Stamina and ki remain hidden during combat
- Injuries use moderate detail
- Limb loss is logically possible
- Death vs knockout is logic-based
- Surrender is personality-based
- Destruction is huge and anime-scale
- Civilian collateral is possible
- Friendly fire is fully simulated
- Surprise attacks matter greatly
- Stealth has moderate value
- Speed gaps are major mechanics
- Technique matchups matter, but raw power remains dominant
- Saiyans and adaptable fighters can evolve frequently during battle

## Growth and Training

- Balanced base growth
- Skill can overcome moderate power gaps
- Realistic plateaus
- Mild overtraining penalties
- Rest matters
- Nutrition is not tracked
- Moderate sparring risk
- Solo training remains viable
- Creative and extra training are rewarded
- Multitasking techniques slows progress
- Skills decay slowly if unused
- Mentors may refuse based on relationships
- Conflicting philosophies are build-defining
- Peaceful-year growth is fully simulated

## Techniques and Transformations

- Original techniques allowed through training
- Technique names are mostly serious
- Copying is talent-based
- Mastery is highly detailed
- Learning misfires are possible
- Unstable forms may fail
- Transformations can significantly affect personality
- Mastery can nearly eliminate drain
- Forms cannot be permanently lost
- Broad custom paths are allowed
- Forms may be combined through training
- Rage may sometimes create permanent growth
- Tail regrowth stays canon-like
- Great Ape control is trainable
- Divine ki cannot appear absurdly early

## NPCs and Relationships

- NPCs train and evolve independently
- NPCs surpass Goku only when logically earned
- Transformations are dynamic
- NPCs can leave, betray, argue, and form unexpected relationships
- Relationships decay slowly without contact
- Jealousy and grudges are tracked lightly/moderately
- Forgiveness is context-based
- Rivalries are a major growth engine
- Hero-villain friendships are strongly possible
- Children inherit deeply simulated traits

## Canon and Content

- Broken canon events sometimes receive equivalent replacements
- Nearly every later Dragon Ball movie should be adapted when possible
- Original Dragon Ball theatrical retellings are excluded
- Best filler arcs may be included
- Game-original, Heroes, and Xenoverse arcs are generally excluded
- GT is expected unless made impossible
- Super and GT may merge carefully
- Daima consequences may be rewritten when useful
- Villains may appear early when logical
- Canon villain returns are generally preferred
- Replacement villains may be created when arcs collapse
- Playable alternate timelines are rare
- Future knowledge changes events moderately
- Canon endings receive some protection

## Death, Wishes, and Fractures

- Goku's permanent death is reserved for near-endgame situations
- Resurrection quests are only sometimes played fully
- Repeated Dragon Ball hunts are abbreviated
- Wish wording matters
- Shadow Dragon risk accumulates after excessive wish use
- Permanent soul erasure is extremely rare
- Dead NPC development is fully simulated
- Villains may retain bodies when logical
- Resurrection trauma is minimal
- Resurrection restores physical injuries
- Timeline Fractures create moderate anomalies and may rarely fail
- Additional Fractures are rarely earned
- Rewind memory depends on the fracture

## World Simulation

- Economy, travel logistics, and normal politics are light
- Frieza Force is simulated in extreme detail
- Galactic Patrol is a major faction
- Capsule Corp inventions require development time
- Technology may threaten stronger fighters in specialized cases
- Public opinion, fame, and tournament reputation matter
- Villain organizations recruit dynamically
- Planets evolve deeply and independently
- Wars without Goku are rare
- Background characters may become important
- Factions remember actions strongly

## GitHub Operations

- Update GitHub after every turn
- Update character files after every meaningful stat or relationship change
- Use frequent commits
- Use branches for alternate timelines
- Preserve deprecated plans and full branch history
- Run strict multi-file consistency checks before every campaign reply
- Stop play and fix contradictions immediately
- Briefly explain and correct mistakes
- Player may always challenge rulings
- Final archive becomes a massive encyclopedia with statistics

### Hidden-System Interpretation

Because all GitHub files remain player-readable and no separate hidden-DM files are used, hidden stats are hidden from normal narration, not inaccessible to the player. Full hidden-stat summaries are formally presented after each saga.

## Early Dragon Ball

- Begin shortly before Bulma hits Goku with her car
- Use Grandpa Gohan flashbacks at important moments
- Grandpa Gohan's death may eventually be investigated
- Goku does not remember prior Great Ape transformations
- First Dragon Ball hunt stays close to canon pacing
- Random side adventures are rare
- Early enemies may return stronger
- Pilaf Gang are competent comedy villains
- Oolong and Roshi remain close to canon portrayals
- Bulma's gun use is genuinely dangerous but low-consequence
- Learning ordinary society is a major Goku progression system
- Tail weakness remains severe
- Great Ape incidents have meaningful consequences
- First wish is fully player-dependent

## Story Preparation

- Prewrite each saga as a near-complete scenario map
- Prepare the next saga in detail before the current one ends
- Decide extensive hidden future plots in advance
- Randomly generated side adventures are rare
- Major plots may still emerge from play
- Planned villains are rarely swapped secretly
- Villains may learn and improve after observing Goku
- Villains receive plot protection until their intended arcs
- Important NPCs receive limited plot protection
- Random ordinary encounters cannot kill important characters
- Always warn about extreme danger and massive divergence
- Sometimes offer foolish options and risk labels
- Deny physically impossible actions immediately

## Long-Term Direction

- Power ceiling may exceed canonical gods
- Goku may reject canon forms and techniques
- Goku may develop a unique signature fighting style
- Other universes and Demon Realm receive limited dedicated arcs
- Earth remains the emotional home
- Key peaceful-life scenes are played
- Parenting and family life become major simulation systems
- Goku's children may differ completely from canon
- Player may influence names
- Original major villains and supporting characters are heavily included
- No fixed final threat
- Campaign may continue indefinitely
- Ultimate goal: build an enormous living universe where Dragon Ball canon is only the beginning
