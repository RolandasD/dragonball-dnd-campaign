# Locked Campaign Rules

These rules define the campaign's non-negotiable foundation.

## Campaign Scope

The campaign may draw from:

- Dragon Ball
- Dragon Ball Z
- Dragon Ball Super
- Dragon Ball GT
- Dragon Ball Daima
- Movies
- What-if arcs

## Campaign Start

The campaign begins at the true start of original Dragon Ball:

- Kid Goku living alone
- Bulma meeting Goku
- The first Dragon Ball hunt beginning

## Canon Philosophy

- Canon skeleton stays unless major divergences logically break it.
- Canon events are treated as gravity, not chains.
- Small changes bend the timeline.
- Major changes break the timeline.
- Canon purpose matters more than exact canon execution.

Example:

- Raditz arriving should reveal Saiyans and raise stakes.
- If Raditz survives, those purposes can still happen through a changed path.

## Player Perspective

- The player controls Goku only.
- Temporary perspective swaps should be rare and only used for clarity, dramatic cutaways, or consequences.
- Other characters should act independently according to personality, knowledge, motivation, and hidden state.

## Power Level Logic

- Power levels matter heavily.
- Massive power differences must be respected.
- Weak characters cannot magically keep up without a logical reason.
- A character with a huge PL disadvantage is normally overwhelmed.

Allowed exceptions:

- teamwork
- strategy
- environmental advantage
- surprise
- special techniques
- rage boosts
- transformations
- exhaustion or injury
- opponent arrogance

## Difficulty

- Difficulty is challenging.
- Bad choices can hurt relationships, cost opportunities, cause injuries, or cause deaths.
- The campaign should not be unfair, but it should not protect the player from logical consequences.

## Tone

- Use TFS-style flavor humor.
- Do not turn the entire campaign into clown mode.
- Stakes remain serious.
- Characters should stay true to their personalities.
- Humor should sharpen scenes, not destroy continuity.

## Gameplay Style

- Guided sandbox.
- Scenes should usually provide numbered options plus an open-ended option.
- The player can always choose something not listed.
- Choices must have consequences.
- Sessions are expected to be long and chaotic.

## Default Choice Format

Use something like:

1. Direct/action option
2. Social option
3. Tactical option
4. Training/observation option
5. Weird/comedy option
10. Something else

## Death Rules

- Death matters.
- Dragon Balls are limited and not an automatic undo button.
- Revivals must respect available Dragon Balls, cooldowns, rules, and consequences.

## Rewind Rules

- Limited rewinds are allowed through Timeline Fractures.
- Default: 1 major Timeline Fracture per saga.
- Timeline Fractures are for major regrets, not tiny dialogue optimization.
- Rewinds may leave echoes, instincts, deja vu, or subtle consequences.

## Training Rules

- Training matters heavily.
- Growth should feel earned.
- Training is a full RPG simulation, not a fixed canon ladder.
- Training results depend on choices, mentor quality, effort, rest, mindset, body condition, combat experience, and hidden traits.

## RNG Philosophy

- Logic matters more than randomness.
- RNG can affect uncertain situations, close fights, training details, and social outcomes.
- RNG should not override obvious power gaps or obvious consequences.

## Visibility Philosophy

- The player sees major useful stats.
- Deep simulation systems and hidden NPC variables may remain hidden.
- Visible stats should support roleplay, not turn the campaign into pure spreadsheet play.

## Cards

- No card system for this campaign unless later explicitly added.
