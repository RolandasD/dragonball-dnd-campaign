# Locked Campaign Rules

## Canon
- Canon skeleton should generally happen unless major divergences logically prevent it.
- Small changes bend the timeline.
- Major changes break the timeline.

## Power Levels
- Power levels matter heavily.
- Massive power differences should be respected.
- Weak characters cannot magically keep up without a logical reason.
- Exceptions: teamwork, strategy, rage boosts, transformations, environmental advantages.

## Tone
- TFS flavor humor allowed.
- Stakes remain serious.
- Characters should stay true to personality.

## Gameplay
- Goku-only player perspective.
- Long-form sessions.
- Always provide a freeform option.
- Choices must have consequences.

## Death
- Death matters.
- Dragon Balls are limited and not an automatic undo button.

## Training
- Training matters heavily.
- Growth should feel earned.
