# Advanced Simulation Systems

## Time Progression
Mode: B (Semi-Simulated)

- Training takes meaningful time.
- Travel takes meaningful time.
- Character growth happens while time passes.
- NPCs evolve in the background.
- Major story pacing should still feel cinematic.

The campaign should avoid boring realism while preserving meaningful progression.

## Skill Progression
Mode: C (Deep RPG)

Skills evolve independently.

Tracked skill areas may include:

- Martial Arts
- Ki Control
- Combat IQ
- Movement
- Reaction Speed
- Mental Focus
- Survival
- Social Skills
- Adaptation
- Meditation
- Technique Mastery

Growth should be based on:

- mentor quality
- training quality
- repetition
- combat experience
- creativity
- mindset
- injuries
- rest
- emotional condition

## Inventory Realism
Mode: A (Light)

- Keep inventory simple and enjoyable.
- Track important items only.
- Avoid survival-management annoyance.

Examples:

- Dragon Balls
- Senzu Beans
- Capsules
- special gear
- weighted clothing

## Technique Learning
Mode: B (Training Based)

Techniques are earned.

Technique growth depends on:

- teacher quality
- compatibility
- repetition
- understanding
- combat usage
- experimentation

Techniques may improve over time.

Example dimensions:

- mastery
- charge speed
- efficiency
- accuracy
- creativity

## Injury System
Mode: B (Moderate)

Injuries matter.

Possible consequences:

- reduced performance
- slower growth
- emotional impact
- technique limitations
- fatigue

The system should stay fun and avoid excessive punishment.

## Personality Growth
Mode: C (Deep Psychological Evolution)

Characters can meaningfully change.

Growth may happen through:

- trauma
- mentorship
- friendships
- rivalries
- victories
- failures
- responsibility
- loss

Kid Goku may slowly evolve into a different version of himself based on player choices and life experiences.

However:

- personality changes should feel earned
- personality should not randomly flip
- core identity still matters
