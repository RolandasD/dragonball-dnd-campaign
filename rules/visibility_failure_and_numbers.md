# Visibility, Failure & Numerical Rules

## Power-Level Visibility
Mode: C — Always Visible

- Exact power levels should usually be shown for relevant fighters and transformations.
- Suppression does not hide the character's known true maximum from the player-facing presentation.
- Scouters and in-world characters may still be deceived by suppression.

## Stat Update Frequency
Mode: B — Meaningful Moments

Show stat updates after:

- major training
- major battles
- injuries
- transformations
- important discoveries
- saga transitions

Avoid cluttering ordinary dialogue scenes with constant updates.

## Training Feedback
Mode: B — Narrative Plus Key Numbers

Training results should include:

- narrative description
- important power changes
- major skill changes
- technique/mastery progress
- notable injuries or fatigue

Do not expose every hidden modifier.

## Transformation Discovery
Mode: B — Fully Dynamic

- Forms unlock whenever biological, emotional, spiritual, and training requirements are logically met.
- Canon timing is not a hard floor.
- Extremely early transformations still require extraordinary and believable circumstances.

## Failure Philosophy
Mode: B — Fail Forward

- Failure creates consequences and new story paths.
- Failure does not automatically mean meaningless campaign termination.
- Death, lost opportunities, and major damage remain possible.

## Hidden Luck
Mode: B — Small Hidden Luck Factor

- Small luck factors may create occasional clutch moments or unfortunate breaks.
- Luck cannot override overwhelming power gaps or obvious logic.

## Power-Level Contradictions
Mode: B — Simulation Wins

- If the campaign's simulated growth logically makes a character stronger than the benchmark, the simulation result wins.
- Canon benchmark values are reference anchors only.

## Suppressed Ki Presentation
Mode: A — Show Actual Full Power Anyway

- Player-facing stat displays show actual known full power.
- In-world observers, enemies, and scouters may only perceive suppressed power.
