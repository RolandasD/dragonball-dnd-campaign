- Great Ape control readiness: none
- Ki-awareness readiness: dormant
- Kamehameha-copy potential: exceptional
- Social-learning responsiveness: high
- Leadership readiness: dormant
- Transformation readiness beyond Great Ape: none

## NPC Private Motives

### Bulma

- Primary motive: obtain all seven Dragon Balls and wish for an ideal boyfriend
- Secondary motive: prove her independence and competence
- Initial approach to Goku: persuade first, manipulate if convenient, use force only if frightened or frustrated
- Hidden concern: losing or damaging the Dragon Radar

### Master Roshi

- Primary motive: quiet enjoyment and personal entertainment
- Dormant motive: find worthy students who can carry the Turtle School forward
- Goku training-interest threshold: requires witnessing purity, unusual potential, or impressive imitation/adaptation

### Oolong

- Primary motive: preserve comfort and avoid punishment
- Hidden capacity: can become loyal when accepted despite cowardice

### Yamcha

- Primary motive: use the Dragon Balls to eliminate his fear of women
- Hidden capacity: strong loyalty and healthy rivalry if treated with respect
- Hidden insecurity: fears humiliation more than physical defeat

### Pilaf Gang

- Pilaf's primary motive: wish to become ruler of the world
- Mai's private motive: complete the mission competently and protect the team
- Shu's private motive: prove useful and impress Pilaf

## Secret Plans in Motion

- Bulma is following the Dragon Radar signal toward Goku's home.
- Pilaf Gang continues gathering intelligence on Dragon Ball sightings.
- No faction currently knows Goku exists.

## Hidden Relationship Starting Modifiers

- Bulma is likely to gain trust from Goku through honesty, food, adventure, and respect for Grandpa Gohan's keepsake.
- Goku is likely to lose trust in Bulma if she attempts to steal or damage the Four-Star Ball.
- Roshi is highly responsive to demonstrations of martial potential.
- Yamcha rivalry growth accelerates through close, honorable fights.

None. Hidden values guide reactions but do not force outcomes.
