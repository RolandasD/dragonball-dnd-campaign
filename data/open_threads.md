- Goku wants to find Grandpa's Four-Star Ball again when the Dragon Balls reactivate.
- Goku is emotionally interested in reviving Grandpa Gohan someday, but the wish is unavailable for one Earth year.
- Goku now knows the Dragon Balls can summon a wish-granting dragon, scatter after use, and become inactive stone for one year.
- Bulma's boyfriend wish was not granted and her first hunt ended with Oolong wasting the wish.
- Master Roshi is strongly interested in training Goku after seeing his kindness, tail, unusual strength, Grandpa Gohan connection, Nimbus worthiness, and Kamehameha spark.

- Goku transformed into a Great Ape after seeing the full moon, blacked out, damaged Pilaf Castle, and woke with no memory.

- The party knows the full moon/tail situation is dangerous, but Goku still does not understand the full truth.
- Oolong is a reluctant ally after being forced into restitution and accidentally saving the world with his wish.
- Yamcha and Puar are loose allies/followers. Yamcha has a rivalry and self-improvement seed from Goku.
