# Son Goku — Live Character Record

## Identity

- Age: 12 by his own understanding
- Species: Saiyan, unknown to Goku
- Current location: Pilaf Castle ruins, desert region
- Current status: Sore; tail cut off; emotionally subdued after losing Grandpa Gohan's Four-Star Ball
- Current base/current/peak PL: 10 / 10 / 100

## Known Traits

- Pure-hearted
- Direct
- Curious
- Battle-loving
- Self-sufficient
- Extremely naive about society
- Tail weakness currently inactive because tail has been cut off
- No stable conscious ki control
- Can ride Flying Nimbus
- Has no memory of Great Ape actions after transformation

## Skills

- Wilderness Survival: High
- Power Pole Combat: Moderate
- Unarmed Martial Arts: Moderate
- Combat Adaptation: High
- Social Awareness: Very Low, improving through travel
- Battlefield Awareness: Beginner seed from Roshi lesson
- Ki Sensing: None
- Ki Control: Tiny unstable spark only

## Known Techniques

- Power Pole combat basics
- Kamehameha: tiny unstable spark only; not learned or mastered. Goku copied Roshi's stance and produced a small burst after one viewing, but Roshi warned him not to force it without proper training.

## Inventory

- Power Pole: carried
- Flying Nimbus: available; given by Master Roshi
- Four-Star Dragon Ball: lost/scattered/inactive for one year; Grandpa Gohan's keepsake and major emotional priority
- Three-Star Dragon Ball: lost/scattered/inactive for one year

## Relationships

- Grandpa Gohan: deceased guardian; defining love and loyalty; possible future resurrection wish remains emotionally important
- Bulma: travel companion; trust and respect increased after first Dragon Ball hunt, but she now knows Goku's Great Ape form is dangerous
- Turtle: friendly; rescued by Goku
- Master Roshi: strong mentor hook; knew Grandpa Gohan, gave Goku Nimbus, saw Kamehameha spark, and may train Goku after the hunt
- Oolong: reluctant ally; exposed and defeated by Goku, later stopped Pilaf's wish
- Yamcha: early rival/loose ally; defeated by Goku; encouraged by Goku to train and work on fear of women
- Puar: friendly cautious; cut off Goku's tail to stop Great Ape; Goku thanked Puar afterward
- Chi-Chi: positive first bond; Goku rescued her from a dinosaur and confirmed she can ride Nimbus
- Ox-King: grateful ally; Goku saved Chi-Chi and helped resolve Fry-Pan Mountain
- Pilaf Gang: enemies; stole the Dragon Balls with traps and sleeping gas, failed their wish

## Current Knowledge

Goku knows:

- the Four-Star Ball belonged to Grandpa Gohan
- Mount Paozu wilderness
- hunting, fishing, and basic martial arts
- Bulma says seven Dragon Balls can summon a dragon that grants one wish
- Bulma wanted to gather the balls for a boyfriend wish
- the dragon might be able to bring Grandpa Gohan back, but this is uncertain
- Dragon Balls scatter after a wish and become stone/inactive for one year
- Grandpa's Four-Star Ball is now somewhere in the world and cannot be tracked normally until the balls reactivate
- cars are machines, not animals, though he still barely understands them
- the world is much larger than Mount Paozu
- Turtle can talk and has Master Roshi as his master
- Master Roshi trained Grandpa Gohan
- Roshi can use the Kamehameha and destroyed Fry-Pan Mountain's fire with it
- Flying Nimbus accepts pure-hearted riders; Goku and Chi-Chi can ride it
- Oolong and Puar can transform
- Yamcha uses Wolf Fang Fist and is scared of women
- Puar cut off his tail because it stopped something dangerous

Goku does not know:

- his Saiyan origin
- what caused Grandpa Gohan's death
- that he killed Grandpa Gohan as a Great Ape
- modern technology, money, romance, engines, or wider society
- full Dragon Ball resurrection limits
- the true nature of the Great Ape form
- what he did while transformed at Pilaf Castle

## Turn History

- Caught a giant fish on the morning of Age 749, September 1.
- Investigated Bulma's vehicle after being struck by it.
- Met Bulma and inspected her car carefully rather than destroying it.
- Learned the Dragon Balls can summon a wish-granting dragon.
- Agreed to travel with Bulma while keeping the Four-Star Ball himself.
- Said goodbye to Grandpa Gohan's house.
- Ate the giant fish before leaving.
- Left Mount Paozu with Bulma.
- Rode in a car for the first time and called it a metal animal.
- Quietly watched Mount Paozu disappear and realized the world is much bigger than he thought.
- Helped lost Turtle return to the sea by carrying him while running.
- Raced Bulma's car while carrying Turtle and won.
- Met Master Roshi after Turtle brought him to the beach.
- Bowed politely and admitted he rescued Turtle.
- Received the Three-Star Dragon Ball from Roshi after mentioning Grandpa Gohan.
- Learned Roshi knew and trained Grandpa Gohan.
- Received a quick battlefield-awareness lesson from Roshi.
- Received Flying Nimbus from Roshi.
- Helped Aru Village by exposing and defeating Oolong.
- Forced Oolong to apologize and allowed/forced him to travel with the group as useful restitution.
- Met Yamcha and Puar in the desert after detecting their ambush.
- Defeated Yamcha after adapting to Wolf Fang Fist rhythm and using the Power Pole.
- Encouraged Yamcha to keep training and work on his fear of women.
- Pulled Oolong's cart form after Bulma's car overheated.
- Met Ox-King at Fry-Pan Mountain.
- Found and saved Chi-Chi from a dinosaur, then flew with her on Nimbus to find Roshi.
- Watched Roshi use Kamehameha to extinguish Fry-Pan Mountain's fire and destroy most of Ox-King's castle.
- Produced a tiny unstable Kamehameha burst after asking Roshi how it worked.
- Asked Roshi to teach him properly someday.
- Helped recover the Seven-Star Dragon Ball from Fry-Pan Mountain ruins.
- Said goodbye to Ox-King and Chi-Chi.
- Stayed with Bulma and the group while tracking the moving final Dragon Ball.
- Entered Pilaf Castle directly and was trapped.
- Tried to break out with strength and Power Pole before Pilaf used sleeping gas.
- Woke in a prison chamber and yelled for Grandpa's ball back.
- Saw Shenron summoned; Oolong used the wish to stop Pilaf's world-domination wish.
- Learned the Dragon Balls scattered and turned to stone for one year.
- Saw the full moon and transformed into Great Ape, then blacked out.
- Woke in the ruins with no memory of the rampage and his tail cut off.
- Apologized to the group and thanked Puar for cutting his tail after learning it stopped him.
