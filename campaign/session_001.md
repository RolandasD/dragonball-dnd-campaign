# Session 001 — Emperor Pilaf Saga

## Date

Age 749, September 1–10.

## Locations

- Mount Paozu
- Road toward the coast
- Beach near Kame House waters
- Aru Village
- Desert route
- Fry-Pan Mountain
- Roshi beach / Kame House waters
- Pilaf Castle
- Pilaf Castle ruins

## Player Actions and Story Progress

1. Goku caught a giant fish quickly and investigated the strange rumbling.
2. Bulma's vehicle struck Goku on the mountain road. Goku was unharmed.
3. Goku inspected Bulma's car by lifting and shaking it, then put it down gently when told.
4. Goku led Bulma to his house after she asked about a round orange ball with stars.
5. Bulma explained that seven Dragon Balls can summon a dragon to grant one wish.
6. Goku asked whether the dragon could bring Grandpa Gohan back.
7. Goku agreed to travel with Bulma, but kept the Four-Star Ball himself.
8. Goku said goodbye to Grandpa Gohan's house.
9. Goku ate the giant fish before leaving.
10. Goku entered Bulma's car for the first time and called it a metal animal.
11. Bulma explained it is called a car and has an engine.
12. Goku quietly watched Mount Paozu disappear behind him and realized the world is much bigger than he thought.
13. Goku stopped to help a lost Turtle.
14. Goku carried Turtle while running to treat it as exercise and beat Bulma's car in a race while carrying Turtle.
15. Turtle brought Master Roshi to the beach.
16. Goku bowed politely and admitted he rescued Turtle.
17. Goku told Roshi the dragon might bring Grandpa Gohan back.
18. Roshi gave Goku the Three-Star Dragon Ball because of the Grandpa Gohan connection.
19. Goku asked how Roshi knew Grandpa Gohan.
20. Roshi revealed Grandpa Gohan trained under him and was one of his best students.
21. Roshi gave Goku a quick battlefield-awareness lesson and became more interested in training him later.
22. Roshi gave Goku the Flying Nimbus. Goku can ride it; Bulma and Oolong cannot.
23. The party traveled to Aru Village.
24. Aru villagers revealed they possessed the Six-Star Dragon Ball and offered it as payment for stopping Oolong.
25. Oolong appeared as the village threat. Goku immediately suspected the monster was fake because it smelled like a pig.
26. Goku exposed Oolong as a pig shapeshifter by smell, tested him with a rock, and defeated him quickly.
27. Oolong admitted he wanted servants. Goku forced him to apologize, and Bulma/Goku pulled him into the group as useful punishment.
28. The party obtained the Six-Star Dragon Ball.
29. In the desert, Goku detected Yamcha and Puar before their ambush. Yamcha's fear of women was exposed immediately.
30. Yamcha did not have a Dragon Ball, tried to rob the party, fought Goku, and lost partly because Bulma's presence distracted him.
31. Goku asked for a proper rematch. During the fight, Goku briefly copied the rhythm of Wolf Fang Fist and countered after reading Yamcha's pattern.
32. Goku finished the first Yamcha fight by using the Power Pole to vault over Yamcha and trip him during Wolf Fang Fist.
33. Puar revealed Yamcha wanted the Dragon Balls to wish away his fear of women.
34. Goku encouraged Yamcha to keep training and work on his fear instead of wasting his wish. Yamcha took it seriously, though he pretended not to.
35. Bulma's car overheated in the desert. Oolong transformed into a cart with a canopy while Goku pulled it.
36. Oolong confirmed his transformation lasts about five minutes before cooldown. The party found a small shaded water pocket and refilled limited water supplies.
37. Goku politely introduced the group to Ox-King at Fry-Pan Mountain.
38. Ox-King revealed Chi-Chi went to find Roshi, the castle was burning, and the Seven-Star Dragon Ball was inside.
39. Goku revealed Nimbus to Ox-King. Ox-King trusted him more and asked him to find Chi-Chi.
40. Goku found Chi-Chi near Fry-Pan Mountain, saved her from a dinosaur, confirmed she can ride Nimbus, and brought her into the story.
41. Instead of returning straight to Ox-King, Goku and Chi-Chi flew on Nimbus to find Roshi and ask him to extinguish Fry-Pan Mountain's fire.
42. Goku brought Chi-Chi safely back to Ox-King before Roshi arrived.
43. Goku inspected Fry-Pan Mountain from Nimbus, confirmed the flames were too dangerous to enter, checked on Chi-Chi, and Roshi arrived to use the Kamehameha.
44. Goku moved everyone back before Roshi fired the Kamehameha, watched carefully, copied the stance, and gained a learning seed for the Kamehameha.
45. Roshi destroyed the fire and most of Ox-King's castle.
46. After seeing Roshi's Kamehameha once, Goku asked how it worked and produced a tiny unstable burst. Roshi was shocked; Goku has not mastered it.
47. Goku apologized to Ox-King, nearly tried Kamehameha again, but listened when Roshi warned him. Goku asked Roshi to teach him properly someday, and Roshi said he would consider it after the Dragon Ball hunt.
48. The party recovered the Seven-Star Dragon Ball from Fry-Pan Mountain ruins.
49. Ox-King may need rebuilding help later. Goku and Chi-Chi had a positive first bond.
50. Before leaving Fry-Pan Mountain, Goku asked Roshi about future training, noticed Yamcha/Puar nearby, said goodbye to Ox-King and Chi-Chi, and Bulma confirmed the final Dragon Ball was moving.
51. Yamcha and Puar agreed to help follow the moving final Dragon Ball. Goku chose to stay with Bulma and the group instead of flying ahead.
52. The group approached a strange desert castle directly.
53. Pilaf revealed himself as holder of the final Dragon Ball and trapped the group inside.
54. Inside Pilaf's castle, Goku dented the trap wall, discovered a movable ceiling panel with the Power Pole, and nearly reached Pilaf's balcony before shutters blocked him.
55. Pilaf used sleeping gas after the group began damaging the trap room. The party was knocked unconscious and the Dragon Balls were stolen.
56. Goku yelled for Grandpa's ball back while Oolong and Puar transformed to search for an escape gap.
57. Shenron was summoned before the party escaped.
58. Oolong stopped Pilaf's world-domination wish by wishing for panties.
59. The Dragon Balls scattered and became inactive for one year, preventing Goku from reviving Grandpa Gohan for now.
60. After Oolong's wish, Goku asked where Grandpa's Four-Star Ball went. Bulma explained the Dragon Balls scattered and turned to stone for one year.
61. Goku saw the full moon, beginning the Great Ape trigger.
62. Goku transformed into Great Ape. From Goku's perspective, he blacked out and woke after the destruction.
63. Puar cut off Goku's tail to stop the transformation. Goku has no memory of the rampage.
64. After waking from the Great Ape incident, Goku apologized despite having no memory. He thanked Puar after learning cutting his tail stopped him.
65. Goku asked if everyone was okay after the aftermath.
