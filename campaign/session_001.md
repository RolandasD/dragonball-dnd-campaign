# Session 001 — Emperor Pilaf Saga Opening

## Date

Age 749, September 1 — morning.

## Locations

- Mount Paozu
- Road toward the coast
- Beach near Kame House waters

## Player Actions and Story Progress

1. Goku caught a giant fish quickly and investigated the strange rumbling.
2. Bulma's vehicle struck Goku on the mountain road. Goku was unharmed.
3. Goku inspected Bulma's car by lifting and shaking it, then put it down gently when told.
4. Goku led Bulma to his house after she asked about a round orange ball with stars.
5. Bulma explained that seven Dragon Balls can summon a dragon to grant one wish.
6. Goku asked whether the dragon could bring Grandpa Gohan back.
7. Goku agreed to travel with Bulma, but kept the Four-Star Ball himself.
8. Goku said goodbye to Grandpa Gohan's house.
9. Goku ate the giant fish before leaving.
10. Goku entered Bulma's car for the first time and called it a metal animal.
11. Bulma explained it is called a car and has an engine.
12. Goku quietly watched Mount Paozu disappear behind him and realized the world is much bigger than he thought.
13. Goku asked where the next Dragon Ball was. Bulma said the next signal was toward the coast.
14. Goku stopped to help a lost Turtle.
15. Goku asked how Turtle got lost, then declared that helping comes before Dragon Balls.
16. Goku carried Turtle while running to treat it as exercise.
17. Goku raced Bulma's car while carrying Turtle and won.
18. Turtle returned to the sea and later brought Master Roshi to the beach.
19. Goku bowed politely and admitted he rescued Turtle.
20. Bulma asked about Roshi's Dragon Ball.
21. Goku told Roshi the dragon might bring Grandpa Gohan back.
22. Roshi gave Goku the Three-Star Dragon Ball.
23. Goku asked how Roshi knew Grandpa Gohan.
24. Roshi revealed Grandpa Gohan trained under him and was one of his best students.
25. Goku said Grandpa Gohan died and that he does not remember how.
26. Goku asked about Grandpa's training and whether Grandpa was stronger than Roshi.
27. Roshi explained the Turtle School philosophy and asked how much Grandpa Gohan taught Goku.
