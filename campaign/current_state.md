## Immediate Situation
The first Dragon Ball hunt reached its climax at Pilaf Castle. Pilaf used traps and sleeping gas to knock the party unconscious and steal the six Dragon Balls they carried. Shenron was summoned, but Oolong stopped Pilaf's world-domination wish by making a useless underwear wish. The Dragon Balls scattered and became inactive for one year, preventing Goku from reviving Grandpa Gohan for now. Goku then saw the full moon, transformed into a Great Ape, and blacked out. From Goku's perspective, he woke in the damaged ruins with no memory of the rampage. Puar cut off Goku's tail to stop the transformation. Goku apologized to the group and thanked Puar after learning what happened.
