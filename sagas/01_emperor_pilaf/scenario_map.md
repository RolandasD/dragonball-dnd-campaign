# Emperor Pilaf Saga — Scenario Map

## Purpose

Introduce Goku to the wider world, establish Bulma as his first travelling companion, begin the Dragon Ball hunt, expose Goku to ordinary society, and end with the first major Great Ape incident and the campaign's first wish.

## Status

Completed in play. See `campaign/session_001.md` and `campaign/current_state.md` for the saved outcome.

## Canon Gravity

The saga should normally preserve these pillars unless player choices logically prevent them:

1. Bulma meets Goku and identifies the Four-Star Dragon Ball.
2. Goku leaves Mount Paozu for the first time.
3. Turtle is rescued and Master Roshi is met.
4. Goku receives the Flying Nimbus if he proves worthy.
5. Oolong is confronted and may join the group.
6. Yamcha and Puar are encountered.
7. Fry-Pan Mountain and the Ox-King are reached.
8. Pilaf gathers or steals the Dragon Balls.
9. Shenron is summoned.
10. Goku transforms into a Great Ape under the full moon.

All ten pillars occurred in the played timeline.

## Opening State

- Date: Age 749, September 1.
- Location: Mount Paozu.
- Goku lives alone with Grandpa Gohan's Four-Star Dragon Ball.
- Goku has never met Bulma and knows almost nothing about cities, technology, romance, money, or the wider world.
- Bulma possesses the Two-Star and Five-Star Dragon Balls and is tracking the Four-Star Ball.
- Emperor Pilaf possesses the One-Star Dragon Ball and is searching for the remaining balls.

## Played Outcome Summary

- Goku met Bulma and left Mount Paozu while keeping ownership of the Four-Star Ball.
- Goku rescued Turtle and met Master Roshi.
- Roshi gave Goku the Three-Star Dragon Ball emotionally because of the Grandpa Gohan connection.
- Roshi gave Goku a quick battlefield-awareness lesson and Flying Nimbus.
- Aru villagers owned the Six-Star Ball and rewarded the party after Goku exposed/defeated Oolong.
- Oolong was forced to apologize and became a reluctant ally.
- Goku detected Yamcha and Puar before the ambush, fought Yamcha, adapted to Wolf Fang Fist, and won with the Power Pole.
- Goku encouraged Yamcha to train and work on his fear of women.
- Bulma's car overheated; Oolong's transformation duration was confirmed around five minutes.
- Goku met Ox-King and rescued Chi-Chi from a dinosaur.
- Goku and Chi-Chi flew on Nimbus to find Roshi.
- Roshi extinguished Fry-Pan Mountain's fire with Kamehameha and destroyed most of the castle.
- Goku watched closely and produced a tiny unstable Kamehameha burst after one viewing.
- The party recovered the Seven-Star Ball from the ruins.
- Pilaf revealed himself after the party approached his castle directly.
- Pilaf trapped the group, used sleeping gas, stole the Dragon Balls, and summoned Shenron.
- Oolong stopped Pilaf's wish by wishing for panties.
- The Dragon Balls scattered and turned to stone for one year.
- Goku saw the full moon, transformed into Great Ape, and blacked out.
- Puar cut off Goku's tail to end the transformation.
- Goku woke with no memory, apologized to the group, and thanked Puar.

## Saga End States

- First wish: Oolong wished for panties, preventing Pilaf's world-domination wish.
- Dragon Balls: scattered worldwide and inactive/stone for one Earth year.
- Four-Star Ball: lost/scattered; emotionally important to Goku.
- Goku/Bulma trust: improved, but Bulma now knows Goku's Great Ape danger.
- Oolong loyalty: reluctant ally; restitution ongoing; saved the world accidentally.
- Yamcha rivalry/friendship: rivalry and self-improvement seed active; loose ally/follower.
- Puar loyalty: still loyal to Yamcha; also thanked by Goku for cutting the tail.
- Roshi interest: strong training hook.
- Chi-Chi relationship seed: positive first bond.
- Ox-King: grateful, but castle largely destroyed.
- Pilaf Gang: failed, castle damaged, likely escaped with grudge.
- Great Ape: occurred; castle damage; no confirmed party deaths; Goku has no memory.
- Timeline Fracture availability: 1 remaining, unused.
- Divergence level: 2.

## Protected Future Hooks
