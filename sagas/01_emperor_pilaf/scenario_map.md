# Emperor Pilaf Saga — Scenario Map

## Purpose

Introduce Goku to the wider world, establish Bulma as his first travelling companion, begin the Dragon Ball hunt, expose Goku to ordinary society, and end with the first major Great Ape incident and the campaign's first wish.

## Canon Gravity

The saga should normally preserve these pillars unless player choices logically prevent them:

1. Bulma meets Goku and identifies the Four-Star Dragon Ball.
2. Goku leaves Mount Paozu for the first time.
3. Turtle is rescued and Master Roshi is met.
4. Goku receives the Flying Nimbus if he proves worthy.
5. Oolong is confronted and may join the group.
6. Yamcha and Puar are encountered.
7. Fry-Pan Mountain and the Ox-King are reached.
8. Pilaf gathers or steals the Dragon Balls.
9. Shenron is summoned.
10. Goku transforms into a Great Ape under the full moon.

The exact wish, alliances, casualties, and aftermath are fully dynamic.

## Opening State

- Date: Age 749, September 1.
- Location: Mount Paozu.
- Goku lives alone with Grandpa Gohan's Four-Star Dragon Ball.
- Goku has never met Bulma and knows almost nothing about cities, technology, romance, money, or the wider world.
- Bulma possesses the Two-Star and Five-Star Dragon Balls and is tracking the Four-Star Ball.
- Emperor Pilaf possesses the One-Star Dragon Ball and is searching for the remaining balls.

## Act I — The Mountain Boy

### Scene 1: Morning Hunt

Purpose:
- Establish Goku's daily life, strength, innocence, tail weakness, food priorities, and bond with Grandpa Gohan's keepsake.

Likely events:
- Goku hunts or fishes for breakfast.
- Optional flashback to Grandpa Gohan.
- Bulma's vehicle approaches Mount Paozu.

Divergence risks:
- Goku may avoid Bulma, attack her vehicle, damage the Dragon Radar, or refuse to discuss the Four-Star Ball.

### Scene 2: Bulma's Arrival

Purpose:
- First contact between Goku and the modern world.
- Establish Bulma's intelligence, impatience, ambition, and practical manipulation.

Canon-equivalent outcome:
- Goku agrees to travel while retaining ownership of the Four-Star Ball.

Failure-forward outcomes:
- Bulma earns trust more slowly.
- Goku follows out of curiosity rather than agreement.
- The Dragon Radar is damaged and must be repaired.
- Bulma temporarily leaves but returns with a better approach.

## Act II — The Road Opens

### Scene 3: First Night Away

Purpose:
- Introduce capsules, houses, bathing, beds, food storage, and Goku's lack of ordinary social knowledge.
- Begin Bulma/Goku trust tracking.

### Scene 4: Turtle on the Road

Purpose:
- Test Goku's kindness and Bulma's patience.
- Introduce the coast and Master Roshi.

Possible outcomes:
- Turtle is returned safely.
- Delays alter future encounters.
- Roshi rewards Goku differently depending on conduct.

### Scene 5: Master Roshi

Canon gravity:
- Roshi gives the Three-Star Dragon Ball.
- Nimbus may accept Goku.

Dynamic elements:
- Roshi's opinion of Goku.
- Bulma's willingness to negotiate.
- Whether Goku sees or attempts the Kamehameha.

## Act III — The Terrible Oolong

### Scene 6: Village Under Threat

Purpose:
- First real social problem Goku can solve.
- Introduce deception and transformation abilities.

Dynamic outcomes:
- Oolong is defeated, exposed, befriended, frightened away, or forced into restitution.
- Villagers may reward the group with the Six-Star Ball.
- Oolong's future loyalty depends on treatment.

## Act IV — Desert Rivals

### Scene 7: Yamcha and Puar

Purpose:
- Introduce a near-equal early rival.
- Establish hunger, fatigue, terrain, and surprise attacks as meaningful combat factors.

Dynamic outcomes:
- Yamcha may remain enemy, become rival, cooperate early, or develop a grudge.
- Puar may influence the conflict independently.
- Yamcha's fear of women may be exposed differently.

## Act V — Fry-Pan Mountain

### Scene 8: Ox-King and Chi-Chi

Purpose:
- Establish a massive power gap Goku cannot simply ignore.
- Introduce Chi-Chi and the Ox-King.
- Create the first meaningful childhood relationship seed.

### Scene 9: Roshi's Kamehameha

Canon gravity:
- Roshi extinguishes the flames and destroys the mountain/castle.
- The Seven-Star Ball is recovered.

Dynamic elements:
- Goku's relationship with Chi-Chi.
- Property destruction consequences.
- Whether Goku attempts to imitate the Kamehameha.

## Act VI — Pilaf's Castle

### Scene 10: Pilaf's Trap

Purpose:
- Reward or punish group cohesion, caution, and information security.
- Let the competent-comedy Pilaf Gang exploit mistakes.

Dynamic outcomes:
- Dragon Balls stolen fully or partially.
- Group may infiltrate rather than be captured.
- Pilaf, Mai, or Shu may form grudges or respect.

### Scene 11: The First Wish

- The wish is fully player-dependent.
- Pilaf's wish is not protected.
- Wish wording rules apply.
- The result may create the saga's largest divergence.

### Scene 12: Great Ape

Trigger:
- Goku sees the full moon while his tail remains intact.

Rules:
- Great Ape power = base power x10.
- Goku has no control and no memory afterward.
- Destruction, injuries, and public fear have meaningful consequences.
- Important characters have limited plot protection, but the event is genuinely dangerous.

Possible resolutions:
- Tail removal.
- Moon obstruction.
- Restraint or redirection.
- Castle destruction.
- A highly divergent solution established through play.

## Saga End States

Track at minimum:

- First wish and consequences.
- Four-Star Ball ownership/location.
- Goku/Bulma trust.
- Oolong loyalty and restitution.
- Yamcha rivalry/friendship and confidence.
- Puar loyalty.
- Roshi's interest in training Goku.
- Chi-Chi relationship seed.
- Pilaf Gang status and grudge.
- Great Ape casualties/destruction.
- Goku's awareness of the monster incident.
- Timeline Fracture availability.

## Protected Future Hooks

- Roshi training invitation remains likely but not guaranteed.
- Krillin should enter through a logical equivalent if Goku trains under Roshi.
- Pilaf Gang should survive as recurring competent-comedy rivals unless extraordinary logic prevents it.
- Grandpa Gohan mystery remains dormant but available.
