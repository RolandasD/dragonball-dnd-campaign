# Dragon Ball Wish Rules Reference

This file records the user's supplied Dragon Ball wish-system reference.

## Earth's Dragon Balls — Shenron

### Creator and Limits

- Created by Kami and later reactivated/upgraded by Dende.
- Shenron cannot grant wishes beyond the power of his creator.
- Shenron cannot simply kill a being stronger than his creator.
- Wishes are granted according to their wording, though Shenron may clarify consequences or interpret details with limited discretion.

### Wishes and Cooldown

- Originally grants one wish.
- Dende's upgrade later allows up to three wishes, though the exact number may vary by era/media.
- Standard cooldown: one Earth year.
- The creator may alter rules in exceptional circumstances.

### Resurrection

- A person may normally be revived only once by Shenron.
- A different Eternal Dragon may bypass that restriction.

### Negative Energy

- Wishes generate negative energy within the Dragon Balls.
- Excess buildup may contribute to Shadow Dragon events in a GT-style branch.

## Namekian Dragon Balls — Porunga

- Created by Grand Elder Guru and later maintained by successors.
- Grants three wishes per summoning.
- Requires the Namekian language for summoning and wishes.
- Cooldown: one Namekian year, approximately 130 Earth days.
- Earlier versions allow only one person revived per wish.
- Later upgrades allow mass revival.
- The balls do not scatter across a planet after use in the same manner as Earth's set.

## Black Star Dragon Balls — Ultimate Shenron

- Created by the Nameless Namekian before his split.
- Grant extremely powerful wishes, including effects beyond standard Shenron limitations.
- After use, they scatter across the galaxy.
- The planet where the wish was made will be destroyed after one year unless all seven are returned.

## Super Dragon Balls — Super Shenron

- Created by Zalama.
- Planet-sized Dragon Balls.
- Grant one wish per summoning.
- Have no known practical wish limit.
- Require the Divine Language to summon and command.

## Cereal Dragon Balls — Toronbo

- Only two Dragon Balls.
- Do not turn to stone after use.
- Can be reused immediately after being collected again.
- May grant extreme power wishes by exchanging lifespan or applying another cost.

## Demon Realm Dragon Balls

- Used in Dragon Ball Daima.
- Capable of major transformation-related wishes and reversals.
- Exact rules should be determined by the active Demon Realm arc and its guardian conditions.

## Dark Dragon Balls / Other Sets

- Dark Dragon Balls and Dark Shenron use corrupted or dangerous energy.
- They may cause Shadow Dragon-style consequences.
- Other special Dragon Ball sets may appear in movie, game, Heroes, or what-if arcs.

## Campaign Usage Rules

When resolving a wish, check:

1. Which Dragon Ball set is active.
2. Whether the creator/guardian is alive.
3. Whether the set is charged.
4. Exact wording of the wish.
5. Creator-power limitations.
6. Resurrection history.
7. Possible costs, side effects, or timeline consequences.

Wishes should never be used as a lazy continuity reset when their rules do not support it.
