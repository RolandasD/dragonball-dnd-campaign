# Multiverse & Movie-Villain Mechanics

## Universe Structure

- There are 12 universes.
- Twin universes pair to total 13, such as Universe 6 and Universe 7.
- Twin universes may share related races, planets, and concepts while developing differently.

## Universe 6

- Saiyans retain Planet Sadala.
- Universe 6 Saiyans generally lack tails and operate as protectors rather than conquerors.
- Frost presents himself as a peacekeeper while secretly enabling conflict and black-market activity.

## Universe 11

- Strong justice-focused culture.
- Pride Troopers act as major defenders.
- Jiren and Toppo represent extreme mortal power.
- Exact comparisons with their God of Destruction should remain continuity-sensitive.

## Exempt Universes

- Universes 1, 5, 8, and 12 were exempt from the Tournament of Power because of their high mortal levels.
- Mortal level measures overall development and quality, not merely fighting strength.

## Movie Villain Archetypes

### Cooler

- Frieza's older brother in movie continuity.
- More immediately lethal and less inclined to toy with opponents.
- Fifth Form greatly increases size, armor, and combat output.
- Campaign use: treat as a tactical imperial predator rather than simply another Frieza.

### Z-Era Broly

- Legendary Super Saiyan mutation.
- Overwhelming power generation and extreme durability.
- Continuously rising or overflowing ki is a useful campaign mechanic, but literal infinity should not be treated as automatic.
- Low tactical discipline; fights as a brutal, sadistic powerhouse.
- Campaign use: escalating raid-boss threat whose body and mind destabilize as power rises.

### Janemba

- Reality-warping being created from accumulated evil in Other World machinery.
- Uses spatial distortion, portals, and geometric disassembly/teleportation.
- Insults and positive energy harming him are continuity-specific magical weaknesses.
- Campaign use: normal combat logic may fail until his magical structure is understood.

### Turles

- Low-class Saiyan pirate resembling Goku.
- Uses the Tree of Might to drain planetary life and produce power-boosting fruit.
- Campaign use: an enemy who can rapidly increase base power through planetary exploitation rather than training or transformations.

## Campaign Rule

Movie villains are variant archetypes, not guaranteed copies of their films. Their motives, mechanics, and placement must be rewritten to match the active timeline.
