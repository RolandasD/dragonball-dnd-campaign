# Transformation & Multiplier Reference

This file records the user's supplied transformation benchmarks.

## Usage Rule

- Officially established multipliers are strong defaults.
- Unofficial, inferred, GT, Super, movie, and fanon multipliers are reference-only.
- Actual combat output still depends on stamina, mastery, injury, ki control, emotional state, and compatibility.
- Huge late-series multipliers should be used mainly for relative scaling, not blind arithmetic.

## Combat Interpretation

A multiplier does not guarantee full usable output.

Effective output may be reduced by:

- poor mastery
- energy drain
- injury
- emotional instability
- transformation instability
- speed loss
- environmental limitations
- technique inefficiency

Transformations can also provide non-numeric benefits such as instinct, reaction speed, durability, ki control, or regeneration.
