# Master Dragon Ball Chronology Reference

This file is the campaign's master chronology guide. It is a reference, not a hard script. The played timeline may diverge logically.

## Pre-Dragon Ball Foundations

- Year 0: Rymus creates the Multiverse on orders from the Good Supreme Demon King.
- Divine Calendar 41: Zalama creates the Super Dragon Balls.
- 75 million years Before Age: Old Kai is sealed in the Z Sword by Beerus.
- 10 million years Before Age: Moro is defeated and imprisoned.
- 5 million years Before Age: Majin Buu destroys worlds, kills/absorbs Supreme Kais, and is eventually sealed on Earth.
- Thousands of years Before Age: Namekians leave the Demon Realm; Neva remains behind.
- Age 261: Namek is devastated by storms; Guru survives and Katas sends his son to Earth.
- Age 461: The Nameless Namekian becomes Kami after separating from King Piccolo. Mutaito seals King Piccolo.
- Age 470: Kami creates Earth's Dragon Balls.
- Circa Age 550: Saiyans arrive on Planet Plant.
- Age 650: Roshi finds the Three-Star Dragon Ball; early World Martial Arts Tournaments already exist.
- Age 720–730: Saiyan–Tuffle war; Tuffle revenge projects including Baby and Hatchiyack originate.
- Age 731: Saiyans enter King Cold's service.
- Age 732: Vegeta, DBS Broly, and Launch are born; King Cold transfers command to Frieza; Broly is exiled to Vampa.
- Age 734: Goku is born.
- Age 737: Goku is sent to Earth; Planet Vegeta is destroyed by Frieza.

## Original Dragon Ball

### Emperor Pilaf / First Dragon Ball Hunt — Age 749

- September 1: Bulma meets Goku and identifies the Four-Star Dragon Ball.
- September 2: Goku and Bulma meet Roshi; Turtle is returned; Roshi gives the Three-Star Ball and Nimbus.
- September 5: Oolong is defeated and joins; the Six-Star Ball is obtained.
- September 6–7: Yamcha and Puar are encountered and begin following the group.
- September 9: Roshi destroys Fry-Pan Mountain while extinguishing the flames; the Seven-Star Ball is found. Pilaf captures the group, Oolong steals the wish, and Goku transforms into a Great Ape.

### Roshi Training / 21st Tournament — Age 749–750

- September 10: Goku and Krillin become Roshi's students after finding Launch.
- September 14 onward: Seven months of Roshi training.
- May 7, Age 750: 21st World Martial Arts Tournament. Jackie Chun defeats Goku in the final.

### Red Ribbon Army / Baba — Age 750

- May 8–12: Goku battles the Red Ribbon Army, reaches Korin, defeats Tao, destroys the army, and reunites with Grandpa Gohan at Baba's tournament.

### World Training / 22nd Tournament / King Piccolo — Age 750–753

- Goku travels and trains around the world.
- May 7, Age 753: 22nd World Martial Arts Tournament; Tien wins; Krillin is killed; King Piccolo returns.
- May 8–9: Roshi and Chiaotzu die; King Piccolo restores his youth; Goku drinks the Ultra Divine Water, kills King Piccolo, and Piccolo Jr. is born.
- Age 753–756: Goku trains under Kami and Mr. Popo; his tail is permanently removed.

### 23rd Tournament / Marriage — Age 756

- May 7: Goku defeats Piccolo Jr. and wins the tournament; becomes engaged to Chi-Chi.
- May 8: Goku and Chi-Chi marry.
- Age 757: Gohan is born.

## Dragon Ball Z

### Raditz / Saiyan Saga — Age 761–762

- October 12, Age 761: Raditz reaches Earth. Goku and Raditz die to Piccolo's Special Beam Cannon in canon.
- Gohan trains under Piccolo while Earth prepares.
- November 3, Age 762: Vegeta and Nappa arrive. Yamcha, Chiaotzu, Tien, and Piccolo die. Goku battles Nappa and Vegeta; Vegeta escapes in canon.

### Namek / Frieza Saga — Age 762–763

- November 14, Age 762: Bulma, Gohan, and Krillin leave for Namek.
- December 18–24: Namek conflict escalates through Vegeta, Zarbon, Dodoria, Ginyu Force, and Frieza.
- December 24: Goku becomes Super Saiyan; Frieza is defeated; Namek explodes; Earth and Namekian Dragon Balls restore many casualties.

### Trunks / Android / Cell — Age 763–767

- Age 763: Cell arrives from the future and hides underground.
- August, Age 764: Future Trunks arrives, defeats Frieza and King Cold, and warns about the androids.
- Age 767, May 12: Android 19 and 20 appear; 17, 18, and 16 awaken; Cell emerges.
- May 16–17: Cell reaches Perfect Form; Cell Games are announced.
- May 26: Cell Games. Goku sacrifices himself; Gohan becomes SSJ2 and kills Cell.

Potential movie/special insertion zone includes:

- Super Android 13!
- Broly — The Legendary Super Saiyan
- Hatchiyack material
- Bojack Unbound
- History of Trunks alternate-timeline events

### Majin Buu — Age 774

- May 7: 25th World Martial Arts Tournament; Babidi's plan unfolds; Majin Vegeta fights Goku; Majin Buu is released.
- May 7–8: Vegeta sacrifices himself; Goku reveals SSJ3; Gotenks is formed; Super Buu emerges; Ultimate Gohan awakens; Vegito is formed; Kid Buu destroys Earth; Goku defeats Kid Buu with the Super Spirit Bomb.
- Uub is born.

Potential movie insertion zone includes:

- Broly — Second Coming
- Bio-Broly
- Fusion Reborn
- Wrath of the Dragon

## Dragon Ball Daima — Age 775

- Gomah, Degesu, and Neva use Earth's Dragon Balls to turn the heroes into children/babies.
- Goku, Shin, Glorio, Panzy, Vegeta, Piccolo, Bulma, Neva, Majin Kuu, and Majin Duu become involved in Demon Realm conflicts.
- Vegeta uses SSJ3.
- Goku obtains a Daima-era SSJ4 through Neva's power draw.
- Gomah is defeated; the heroes are restored to adulthood.

## Dragon Ball Super

### Beerus / Resurrection F — Age 778–779

- Age 778: Battle of Gods. Goku becomes Super Saiyan God and battles Beerus.
- Age 779: Frieza is revived, trains, reaches Golden Frieza, and attacks Earth.

### Universe 6 / Future Trunks / Tournament of Power — Age 779–780

- Universe 6 tournament occurs.
- Future Trunks returns due to Goku Black and Zamasu.
- Tournament of Power occurs; Goku awakens Ultra Instinct; Android 17 restores erased universes; Frieza is revived.
- DBS Broly occurs after the Tournament of Power.

### Moro / Granolah — Age 780–781

- Moro escapes and threatens New Namek and Earth.
- Goku develops Ultra Instinct further; Vegeta learns Forced Spirit Fission.
- Granolah becomes the strongest through the Cerealian Dragon Balls.
- Vegeta develops Ultra Ego.
- Gas becomes the strongest; Black Frieza appears.

### Super Hero — Age 782–783

- Trunks and Goten operate as Saiyaman X-1 and X-2.
- Dr. Hedo creates Gamma 1, Gamma 2, and Cell Max.
- Piccolo and Gohan defeat Cell Max; Gohan Beast and Orange Piccolo emerge.

### End of Z — Age 784

- 28th World Martial Arts Tournament.
- Goku leaves with Uub to train him.

## Dragon Ball GT Branch

GT is included as a possible branch, not an automatic future.

### Age 789–790

- Pilaf's wish turns Goku into a child.
- Black Star Dragon Ball space journey.
- Baby arc; Earth is destroyed; Piccolo dies.
- Super 17 arc.
- Shadow Dragons arc; Goku and Vegeta become SSJ4; Gogeta appears; Goku leaves with Shenron.

### Far Future

- Goku Jr. and Vegeta Jr. eventually emerge in the GT branch.

## Movie Inclusion Rule

All Dragon Ball movies are available as campaign content.

Each movie should be treated as a potential arc whose placement and survival depend on the current timeline. Do not force movie events when their required setup has been invalidated by divergences.

## Usage Rule

This chronology is a reference for dates, canon sequence, story pillars, and potential arc placement. Current campaign facts and earned divergences always take priority.
