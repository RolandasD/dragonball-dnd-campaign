# World Martial Arts Tournament Rules

This file records the user's supplied tournament reference.

## History and Schedule

- The World Martial Arts Tournament originated from an old festival at the temple that became the tournament grounds.
- Originally held every five years.
- Changed to every three years after the 22nd World Martial Arts Tournament.

## Prizes

Early tournaments:
- Winner receives 500,000 Zeni.

Later tournaments:
- 1st: 10,000,000 Zeni
- 2nd: 5,000,000 Zeni
- 3rd: 3,000,000 Zeni
- 4th: 2,000,000 Zeni
- 5th: 1,000,000 Zeni

## Match Loss Conditions

A fighter loses if they:

- touch outside the ring
- remain down for a ten-count
- give up
- are disqualified

If both fighters are knocked down and neither rises before ten, the match is normally a draw.

For a final-round draw, sudden death may be used: the first fighter to stand fully and declare themselves champion wins.

## Prohibited Actions

- killing the opponent
- weapons
- protective body gear
- teams
- deliberate eye pokes
- deliberate groin attacks

Natural weapons, such as claws, may be allowed.

Accidental fouls may be overlooked by the referee.

## Tournament Structure

### Classic Format

- Indoor preliminaries without an audience.
- Contestants split into groups, commonly eight.
- Preliminary matches have a one-minute limit and may be decided by points.
- Group winners advance to the public tournament.
- Public tournament commonly uses eight fighters:
  - quarter-finals
  - semi-finals
  - final

Fight order is chosen by random draw.

### Later Format

- Punching machine qualifiers may replace preliminary matches.
- Fifteen highest scores advance, with the reigning champion receiving an automatic place.
- Later tournaments may include sixteen fighters.

### Champion Challenge Format

After the Kid Buu era, the tournament winner may earn the right to challenge the returning champion rather than automatically becoming champion.

## Campaign Usage

- Tournament rules should matter tactically.
- Ring-outs can allow weaker but skilled fighters to defeat stronger opponents.
- Killing remains forbidden unless an arc explicitly corrupts or changes tournament rules.
- Villains may enter intending murder, but the world and officials should react logically.
- Tournament formats may evolve over time.

## Ultimate Fighting Division

In far-future settings, an Ultimate Fighting division may allow weapons, armor, robots, and advanced technology.
