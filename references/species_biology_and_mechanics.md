# Species Biology & Mechanics Reference

This file records the user's supplied race mechanics for campaign use.

## Confidence Labels

- **Strong Default**: safe to use as a general campaign rule.
- **Inferred / Extended Lore**: useful, but not universal or fully confirmed across every continuity.
- **Speculative / Fanon**: reference-only unless explicitly adopted later.

---

## Saiyans

### Core Biology — Strong Default

- Warrior race originating from Planet Sadala and later associated with Planet Vegeta in Universe 7.
- Humanoid with prehensile tails, though tails may be removed and appear genetically recessive in later generations.
- Maintain their physical prime for much longer than humans, aging slowly until later life.
- Naturally adapt well to high gravity.

### Innate Mechanics

#### Zenkai / Saiyan Power — Strong Default

- Recovering from severe or near-death injury can increase a Saiyan's base power.
- The boost should require genuine survival circumstances.
- Repeated deliberate abuse becomes less effective.
- The exact increase depends on prior power, severity, recovery quality, age, and story context.

#### Great Ape — Strong Default

- Triggered through Blutz Waves, commonly from a full moon, absorbed through the eyes.
- Requires a functioning tail.
- Default multiplier: x10.
- Usually causes loss of rational control unless trained.

#### Blutz-Wave Threshold — Inferred / Extended Lore

- Reference value: approximately 17 million zeno units.
- Use only when exact scientific measurement becomes relevant.

### Super Saiyan Genetics

#### S-Cells — Inferred / Extended Lore

- High base power and a gentler/tranquil environment may increase S-Cell count.
- Intense emotion may trigger transformation once the biological threshold is reached.
- Treat S-Cells as one hidden factor, not the only requirement.

### Transformation Traits

- SSJ1: major power increase and emotional catalyst.
- SSJ2: sharper state, stronger output, often accompanied by bio-electricity.
- SSJ3: extremely high output with severe ki drain and poor sustainability.
- God Ki paths: achieved through ritual, divine training, or equivalent extraordinary circumstances.

### Campaign Rules

- Transformations must be earned.
- High base power matters.
- Emotional triggers alone are insufficient without biological readiness.
- Training, mastery, and psychological state determine usable output.

## Namekians

### Core Biology — Strong Default

- Green-skinned humanoids with antennae and slug-like biological traits.
- Reproduce asexually through eggs.
- Require water rather than normal food.
- Common broad specializations include Dragon Clan and Warrior Clan.

### Dragon Clan

- Healing, magic, spiritual ability, and Dragon Ball creation.
- Dragon Ball creation depends on sufficient power, knowledge, and appropriate conditions.

### Warrior Clan

- Focused toward combat ability and physical power.

### Regeneration — Strong Default

- Can regenerate limbs and severe body damage.
- Regeneration consumes substantial stamina and ki.
- Repeated regeneration weakens combat performance.
- Survival generally requires the head/critical life core to remain intact.

### Cranial Nucleus — Inferred / Useful Campaign Model

- Treat consciousness and the critical regeneration core as centered in the head.
- This model should govern whether regeneration remains possible after catastrophic damage.

### Other Abilities

- Limb elasticity.
- Gigantification / Great Namekian state.
- Permanent assimilation with another willing Namekian, adding power, knowledge, and memories.

### Demon Clan Mutations — Inferred / Extended Lore

- King Piccolo's offspring may be treated as corrupted/mutated Namekian creations with radically altered anatomy and combat specialization.

## Frieza's Race / Frost Demon Reference

### Naming Note

- "Frost Demon" is a useful fan/extended-lore label, not necessarily the formal canon species name.

### Core Biology — Strong Default

- Extremely durable.
- Can survive in space without breathing.
- Can survive catastrophic bodily damage that would kill most species.
- Frieza and King Cold are exceptional mutants even among their race.

### Suppression Forms — Strong Default

- Their lower numbered forms are suppression states, not natural power-up transformations.
- Their commonly called Final Form is their natural state.
- Suppression forms help control output and conserve energy.

### Full Power State

- 100% Full Power greatly increases output and muscle mass.
- It causes major stamina drain and declining efficiency.

### Golden / Black Evolutions — Strong Default for Frieza

- Training-created evolutionary forms.
- Not automatic racial stages.
- Appearance may be consciously chosen or influenced by the user.

### Bio-Armor / Physical Limiters — Inferred Model

- The body plating may be treated as natural bio-armor.
- Whether it literally restricts ki is an optional campaign interpretation, not a hard universal rule.

## Majins

### Continuity Note

- Majin Buu's exact origin differs across material. For this campaign, use the active chronology/source policy when resolving ancient history.
- A broader Majin race exists mainly through extended lore such as Dragon Ball Online and Xenoverse.

### Core Biology — Strong Default for Buu-Type Majins

- Magical, highly malleable bodies.
- No conventional skeletal structure or normal organs.
- Can stretch, liquefy, divide, and reform from severe damage.

### Regeneration

- Extremely powerful regeneration.
- Effective destruction requires overwhelming energy and near-total vaporization.
- Regeneration slows or becomes less reliable when ki and stamina are heavily depleted.

### Single-Molecule Survival — Speculative / Fanon

- Do not treat literal single-molecule survival as automatic hard canon.
- Use near-total vaporization and remaining magical cohesion as the campaign model.

### Absorption

- Can absorb other beings.
- Absorption may add power, intelligence, memories, traits, and visual elements.

### Transfiguration Magic

- Can transform living or nonliving targets into other forms, commonly sweets.
- Resistance depends on relative power, magic resistance, technique conditions, and story logic.

### Fission

- Majins may split emotional/alignment aspects into separate beings.
- Total power is divided between the resulting entities according to the circumstances.

### Purification — Extended Lore

- Majins may revert toward a feral Kid Buu-like state, losing absorbed traits and emphasizing raw magical power.

## Core People / Shinjin / Kai

### Core Biology — Extended Lore

- Divine beings associated with the World Core and Kaiju Tree.
- Standard Kais and Supreme Kais have different roles and lifespans.

### Standard Kais

- Govern sectors/quadrants.
- Very long-lived.

### Supreme Kais

- Possess divine ki.
- Creation-oriented divine authority.
- Exceptionally long-lived.

### Divine Ki — Strong Default

- Normally cannot be sensed by ordinary mortals without special training or ability.

### Potara Fusion

- Fusion rules depend on participants and era.
- Supreme Kai involvement may alter duration and permanence.
- Check the active campaign's established Potara rules before resolving.

## Earthlings

### Core Strengths — Strong Default

- Highly adaptable.
- Exceptional martial arts creativity.
- Excellent technical ki manipulation despite lower average natural power.
- Major technological innovation.

### Human Advantages

- Specialized techniques can outperform raw power expectations.
- Examples include Destructo Disc, Tri-Beam, Solar Flare, Mafuba, and advanced martial arts.

### Technology

- Capsule systems.
- Cybernetic enhancement.
- Infinite energy androids.
- Bio-Android engineering.

### Campaign Rule

Earthlings should not receive arbitrary biological multipliers, but can remain relevant through training efficiency, specialized techniques, teamwork, technology, and tactical intelligence.

## Bio-Androids / Cell-Type Chimeras

### Core Concept — Strong Default

Artificial organisms built from multiple species and combatants.

### Inherited Mechanics

Potential inherited traits include:

- Saiyan Zenkai-style growth and combat adaptation.
- Namekian regeneration.
- Frieza-race durability and vacuum survivability.
- genetic memory and copied techniques.

### Regeneration

- Use a critical core/nucleus model.
- Regeneration consumes energy and depends on the core surviving.

### Biomass and Absorption

- Some Bio-Androids drain organic life force to increase power.
- Cell specifically requires Androids 17 and 18 for intended major evolutionary stages.

### Campaign Rule

Inherited abilities are not automatically perfect copies. Effectiveness depends on design quality, available cells, developmental stage, and current energy.

## General Species-System Rule

Species biology provides advantages, weaknesses, and possible growth paths. It does not override:

- current power level
- training
- mastery
- stamina
- injury
- psychology
- technique matchup
- campaign continuity

When a claim is labeled inferred or speculative, the DM should not use it as a surprise hard rule against the player without first establishing it in the campaign.
