# Technique Interactions & Power-Gap Rules

This file defines how specialized techniques interact with Dragon Ball's normal power-level stat check.

## Core Combat Rule: The Stat Check

- Ki acts as passive defensive reinforcement.
- If Character A is significantly stronger than Character B, ordinary strikes and ordinary ki blasts from B may do little or no damage.
- The larger the gap, the less conventional attacks matter.
- Specialized techniques can bypass, redirect, stall, seal, blind, or otherwise work around this rule.

## General Exception Rule

A technique may bypass raw durability without bypassing every other defense.

Even when a move ignores passive ki armor, it may still fail because of:

- poor accuracy
- insufficient speed
- active ki barriers
- technique counters
- superior sensing
- interruption during charge-up
- stamina failure
- lack of mastery

## Destructo Disc / Kienzan

### Function

- Condenses ki into a thin, rapidly spinning cutting disc.
- Designed to cut rather than deliver concussive force.

### Against Stronger Opponents

- Strong durability bypass.
- Can injure or sever targets far stronger than the user if it connects cleanly.
- Treat Krillin severing Second-Form Frieza's tail as the benchmark.

### Limits

- Can be dodged.
- Skilled enemies may redirect, catch, or destroy it using precise concentrated ki.
- An actively maintained dense ki barrier may block or shatter it when the power gap is extreme.
- It does not automatically slice every target regardless of circumstance.

### Campaign Classification

**Durability bypass, not guaranteed hit.**

---

## Solar Flare / Taiyoken

### Function

- Converts ki into an intense flash of light.
- Targets vision rather than physical durability.

### Against Stronger Opponents

- Normally works regardless of raw power gap when the target sees the flash.

### Counters

- closing or shielding the eyes in time
- sunglasses or equivalent protection
- fighting without vision through ki sensing or other senses
- anticipating the technique

### Campaign Classification

**Reliable sensory bypass, but avoidable and recoverable.**

---

## Tri-Beam / Kikoho / Neo Tri-Beam

### Function

- Converts enormous amounts of ki and life force into concentrated kinetic pressure.
- Far more costly than ordinary beam techniques.

### Against Stronger Opponents

- Can pin, repel, interrupt, or stall enemies far stronger than the user.
- It may produce little or no direct bodily damage when the power gap is massive.
- Repeated use can temporarily deny movement or disrupt an enemy's action.

### Costs

- extreme stamina drain
- life-force damage
- risk of unconsciousness or death
- declining output with repeated use

### Campaign Classification

**Kinetic control bypass; limited damage bypass.**

---

## Mafuba / Evil Containment Wave

### Function

- Magical sealing technique that draws the target into a prepared container.
- Requires an appropriate container and sealing talisman unless the current version of the technique establishes otherwise.

### Against Stronger Opponents

- Can affect targets vastly stronger than the user.
- Raw power alone does not make a target immune.

### Costs and Risks

- stamina and life-force cost scales with target power and resistance
- massive gaps may kill the user
- the target or another skilled fighter may reverse or redirect the wave
- interruption, missing talisman, damaged container, or poor aim can cause failure

### Campaign Classification

**Magical sealing bypass with extreme execution risk.**

---

## Regeneration

### Namekian / Cell-Type Regeneration

- Rebuilding body parts consumes meaningful ki and stamina.
- A stronger opponent can exploit regeneration by repeatedly inflicting severe damage.
- Regeneration becomes slower or impossible when reserves are exhausted.
- Destroying the established critical core or nucleus prevents recovery.

### Majin Regeneration

- Far less constrained by conventional anatomy.
- Physical fragmentation alone rarely matters.
- Meaningful defeat generally requires overwhelming destruction, magical countermeasures, absorption/fission effects, or near-total vaporization.
- Regeneration may slow when the Majin is severely exhausted or weakened.

### Campaign Clarification

Literal single-molecule survival is not an automatic universal law. Use remaining magical cohesion and near-total destruction as the practical standard.

## Absorption

### Cell-Type Biological Absorption

- Requires physical penetration or successful bodily capture.
- Passive ki armor can prevent the tail needle from piercing a much stronger target.
- Stronger targets may break free before meaningful draining occurs.

### Buu-Type Magical Absorption

- Uses detached body material to engulf and magically contain a target.
- Can threaten targets stronger than Buu if it lands unnoticed or catches them without protection.

### Counters

- sustained tight ki barrier
- detecting and destroying the detached material
- avoiding full enclosure
- specialized magical resistance or escape ability

### Campaign Classification

- Cell absorption: **physical/stat-dependent**
- Buu absorption: **magical containment bypass**

---

## Charging Multipliers

### Core Rule

- Charging compresses and focuses ki into a single attack.
- A charged technique can temporarily produce output significantly above the user's resting or immediately measured battle power.
- Benchmark: Goku's power against Raditz rose sharply while charging the Kamehameha.

### Factors

Charged output depends on:

- technique mastery
- charge duration
- available ki
- emotional state
- physical condition
- willingness to injure oneself
- interruptions

### Limit

Charging does not grant infinite scaling. A weak user still has a maximum safe and maximum suicidal output.

---

## Beam Clashes

### Core Rule

When two beams collide, compare effective attack output rather than only base power.

Effective output includes:

- current power
- charge multiplier
- transformation state
- technique efficiency
- stamina remaining
- emotional surge
- self-damaging overexertion
- allies contributing power

### Outcomes

- stronger beam overwhelms and engulfs the weaker
- near-even beams remain locked until one side gains output or loses stamina
- unstable clashes may detonate between both fighters
- a weaker fighter may win by overcharging or using a more efficient technique, but risks severe injury or death

## Technique Interaction Table

| Technique | Bypasses Power Gap? | Primary Function | Main Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solar Flare | Yes, sensory | Blindness / interruption | Eyes can be closed; sensing bypasses blindness |
| Destructo Disc | Yes, cutting | Durability bypass | Dodge, redirect, precise barrier |
| Mafuba | Yes, magical | Sealing | Huge cost, setup, reversal risk |
| Tri-Beam | Yes, kinetic control | Pinning / stalling | Burns stamina and life force; little damage vs huge gap |
| Buu Absorption | Yes, magical containment | Absorption | Tight ki barrier, detection, escape |
| Cell Absorption | Usually no | Biomass draining | Cannot pierce vastly stronger active defense |

## DM Resolution Priority

When resolving a specialized technique:

1. Determine whether it bypasses passive durability.
2. Determine whether it actually lands.
3. Check counters and active defenses.
4. Apply stamina, life-force, and setup costs.
5. Respect mastery and character knowledge.
6. Apply logical consequences even if the technique succeeds.

A bypass technique should create meaningful opportunities, not erase all combat logic.
