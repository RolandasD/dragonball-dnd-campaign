# Cosmology, Gods & Afterlife Reference

## God Hierarchy

### Planetary Guardian / Kami

- Oversees a single planet.
- Observes from a lookout or equivalent divine seat.
- May manage that planet's Dragon Balls.

### King Kais

- Manage directional quadrants of a universe.
- Serve under a Grand Kai where that structure applies.

### Supreme Kais / Kaioshin

- Gods of creation.
- Seed life and oversee cosmic development.
- Possess divine ki and creation-oriented authority.
- Their life link to a God of Destruction depends on the active continuity and surviving Supreme Kai structure.

### Gods of Destruction

- Maintain balance through destruction.
- Use Hakai and related destructive authority.
- Their role is not simple villainy; they remove threats, stagnation, or failed worlds according to divine judgment.

### Angels

- Attendants and teachers to Gods of Destruction.
- Strictly neutral by law.
- Serious unauthorized intervention risks erasure or punishment.
- Exact consequences depend on angel law and active continuity.

### Grand Minister and Zeno

- Zeno possesses authority to erase universes and timelines.
- Grand Minister manages much of the divine structure and Angel hierarchy.

## Divine Ki

- Normally cannot be sensed by ordinary mortals.
- Requires special training, status, or perception to detect.

## Hakai

- Divine destruction technique capable of erasing matter and, in many circumstances, souls.
- Resistance depends on relative power, mastery, immortality mechanics, and continuity.
- Do not treat every Hakai attempt as guaranteed success.

## Death and the Afterlife

### Check-In Station

- King Yemma judges souls.

### Standard Souls

- Usually lose their physical bodies.
- Are sent to Heaven, Hell, reincarnation, or another appropriate destination.

### Heroic Exceptions

- Exceptional warriors and heroes may keep their bodies to train in Other World.

### Hell

- May be tailored to the soul and continuity.
- Frieza's personalized punishment is a strong example.

## Dead-Body Combat Advantages

- Dead warriors with retained bodies may experience reduced physical fatigue and fewer mortal limitations.
- High-drain forms may be easier to sustain while dead.
- This is not literal unlimited energy in every circumstance.
- The advantage disappears or changes after resurrection.

## Campaign Resolution Rules

When handling death, check:

1. whether the soul is intact
2. who judged or claimed the soul
3. whether the body was retained
4. resurrection eligibility
5. divine interference
6. timeline-specific afterlife rules
7. whether Hakai or another erasure effect prevents revival
