# Source & Continuity Policy

## Core Rule

The campaign uses the full Dragon Ball franchise as reference material, while the played timeline remains governed by logical consequences.

## Included Material

The campaign may draw from:

- Original Dragon Ball
- Dragon Ball Z / Dragon Ball Z Kai
- Dragon Ball Super
- Dragon Ball Daima
- Dragon Ball GT
- All Dragon Ball movies
- TV specials
- Relevant game-original and what-if concepts when useful

## Movies

All movies are included as potential campaign events.

Movie events should be placed into the timeline where they fit best. They are treated as canon opportunities rather than guaranteed events.

A movie event may be:

- played close to its usual placement
- altered by current divergences
- delayed
- combined with another arc
- prevented entirely if the timeline makes it impossible

The DM must not force a movie plot to happen when its required characters, motives, locations, or conditions no longer exist.

## Source Conflicts

When sources contradict each other, use this priority:

1. Current played campaign facts
2. Locked campaign rules
3. Logical consequences of divergences
4. Main canon story skeleton
5. Master chronology reference
6. Movie/special/game material
7. Power-level benchmark reference

## Important Principle

Canon is gravity, not prison.

The campaign should remain recognizably Dragon Ball, but earned divergences must be respected.
