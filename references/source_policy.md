# Source & Continuity Policy

## Core Rule

The campaign uses the Dragon Ball franchise as reference material, while the played timeline remains governed by logical consequences and the locked campaign profile.

## Included Material

The campaign may draw from:

- Original Dragon Ball
- Dragon Ball Z / Dragon Ball Z Kai
- Dragon Ball Super
- Dragon Ball Daima
- Dragon Ball GT
- TV specials
- selected anime filler
- nearly all Dragon Ball Z and later movies
- original campaign villains, characters, and arcs

## Excluded by Default

- original Dragon Ball theatrical retellings
- game-original story arcs
- Dragon Ball Heroes material
- Xenoverse material

Excluded material may only enter later through an explicit new ruling.

## Movies

Nearly all Dragon Ball Z and later movies are potential campaign events.

Movie events should be placed into the timeline where they fit best. They are canon opportunities rather than guaranteed events.

A movie event may be:

- played close to its usual placement
- altered by current divergences
- delayed
- combined with another arc
- prevented entirely if the timeline makes it impossible

The DM must not force a movie plot when its required characters, motives, locations, or conditions no longer exist.

## Source Conflicts

When sources contradict each other, use this priority:

1. Current played campaign facts
2. `rules/campaign_master_profile.md`
3. Other locked campaign rules
4. Logical consequences of divergences
5. Main canon story skeleton
6. Master chronology reference
7. Movie, special, and selected filler material
8. Power-level benchmark reference
9. General lore references

## Important Principle

Canon is gravity, not prison. The campaign should remain recognizably Dragon Ball, but earned divergences must be respected.
