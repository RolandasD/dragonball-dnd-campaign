# Fusion Mechanics Reference

This file defines the campaign's fusion systems.

## General Rule

Fusion creates a new combat entity whose power, personality, techniques, and limitations depend on the method used. Fusion should never be treated as simple addition unless that method explicitly works that way.

## Metamoran Fusion Dance

### Requirements

- Two participants with closely matched height/build.
- Power levels must be synchronized before fusing.
- Dance must be performed accurately and simultaneously.

### Result

- Creates a new temporary being with a blended personality and combined techniques.
- Usually lasts about 30 minutes.
- Extremely high output can shorten the duration.
- Failed dances create weak or unstable forms.

### Mechanical Rule

- Fusion power is far above simple addition.
- The weaker participant must lower power to match the stronger before fusion.
- Mastery of the dance affects reliability.

## Potara Fusion

### Requirements

- Each participant wears one Potara earring on the opposite ear.

### Result

- Creates a new fused being with a blended personality and techniques.
- Fusion duration depends on participants and continuity.

### Duration Rule

- Supreme Kai fusions may be permanent.
- Mortal fusions generally have a time limit.
- Extremely high power output may shorten the duration.
- Active campaign continuity takes priority when exact Potara rules conflict across media.

## Namekian Assimilation

- Permanent.
- One Namekian becomes the host; the other is fully absorbed.
- No new separate fusion persona is created.
- Host gains power, memories, knowledge, and wisdom.
- Cannot normally be reversed.
- Willing participation is the default requirement.

## Majin Absorption

- Forced biological/magical fusion.
- Majin gains power, intelligence, techniques, and visual traits from absorbed targets.
- Victims remain trapped within the Majin.
- Defusion can occur if victims are physically removed or if special circumstances force separation.
- Exact power gain is influenced by target power, compatibility, and the Majin's current state; do not assume perfectly clean arithmetic in every case.

## Campaign Resolution Rules

When resolving fusion, track:

1. method
2. compatibility
3. synchronization
4. time limit
5. stamina drain
6. personality blend
7. unlocked techniques
8. defusion conditions
9. post-fusion exhaustion

Fusion should feel transformative and powerful, but its setup and limitations must matter.
