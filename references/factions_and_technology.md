# Factions & Technology Reference

## Capsule Corporation

### Core Technology

- Spatial compression through capsules.
- Can store vehicles, buildings, supplies, and complex machinery in compact form.

### Key Innovations

- gravity chambers
- time machines
- advanced spacecraft
- Dragon Radar
- android and reactor-related technology through Earth science networks

### Time Travel Rule

- Traveling to the past and changing events creates a new branch timeline.
- It does not overwrite the traveler's original timeline.

### Campaign Use

- Capsule Corp can solve logistical problems, but inventions require time, expertise, resources, and available data.

## Frieza Force

### Structure

- Galactic military and planetary-conquest organization.
- Uses biological elites, mercenaries, mass-produced soldiers, and advanced logistics.

### Key Technology

#### Scouters

- Read numeric battle power.
- Vulnerable to ki suppression and sudden spikes.
- May overload or explode under extreme readings.
- Heavy reliance on scouters makes users poor at natural ki sensing.

#### Healing Tanks

- Repair severe cellular damage rapidly.
- Recovery time depends on injury severity, species, and available technology.

### Campaign Use

- The Frieza Force should behave like a functioning empire with ranks, communications, bases, supply routes, and political consequences.

## Galactic Patrol

### Role

- Universal law-enforcement organization.
- Many members are not elite fighters and rely on technology, coordination, and specialized agents.

### Key Technology

- blasters
- binding gels
- containment tools
- spacecraft
- timeline-monitoring equipment where continuity permits

### Campaign Use

- The Patrol can investigate, arrest, negotiate, contain, or request help rather than solve everything through raw combat.

## Demon Realm

### Core Traits

- Separated from the outside universes through magical geography, gates, and tunnels.
- Relies heavily on curses, artifacts, magical creatures, and specialized items.
- Political control may depend on surveillance, magic, and institutional authority rather than raw strength alone.

### Campaign Use

- Demon Realm threats should not always obey normal ki-combat assumptions.
- Magical rules, artifacts, and local politics matter.

## General Technology Rule

Technology can bypass power gaps through preparation, containment, information, or specialized tools, but it should not become effortless magic. Track:

1. inventor knowledge
2. construction time
3. resources
4. power source
5. reliability
6. countermeasures
7. whether the technology has seen the target ability before
