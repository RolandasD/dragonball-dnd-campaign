# Movie Placement Guide

This file records the user's supplied placement analysis for Dragon Ball Z movies.

## General Rule

All movies are available as campaign arcs, but their exact timing may be adapted. If a movie conflicts with the active timeline, rewrite its setup rather than forcing contradictions.

## Dead Zone

Best placement: shortly before Raditz.

Useful setup:
- Goku and Piccolo are still enemies.
- Gohan's hidden potential is not yet understood.
- Garlic Jr. seeks immortality and revenge tied to Kami.

Known contradiction:
- Krillin, Bulma, and Roshi already know Gohan.

Campaign treatment:
- Easy to adapt into the main timeline before Raditz.

## The World's Strongest

Original release-era placement conflicts with Namek events.

Campaign treatment:
- Best adapted into the three-year Android training gap or another period when the team is alive and available.
- Goku's technique set and power should be adjusted to match the campaign date.

## The Tree of Might

Original release-era placement conflicts with Namek events.

Campaign treatment:
- Best adapted into the three-year Android training gap or another Earth-based period.
- Turles and the Tree of Might can be rewritten around current Saiyan knowledge and power levels.

## Lord Slug

Original release-era placement conflicts with Namek events.

Campaign treatment:
- Best adapted into the three-year Android training gap or another suitable Earth period.
- Pseudo Super Saiyan may be retained as a temporary emotional transformation or replaced by a timeline-appropriate state.

## Cooler's Revenge

Best placement:
- After Namek and before the Androids, usually during the three-year training period.

Known issue:
- Cooler attributes Frieza's defeat to Goku even when Future Trunks kills Mecha Frieza.

Campaign treatment:
- Cooler's information and motive should reflect what actually happened in the played timeline.

## The Return of Cooler

Possible placement:
- Around the Cell era, often adapted into the ten-day wait before the Cell Games or another suitable period.

Known issues:
- Dende is Guardian.
- Vegeta is Super Saiyan.
- Goku and Gohan behavior does not perfectly match the canon Cell Games period.

Campaign treatment:
- Rewrite the exact timing according to current state.

## Super Android 13

Possible placement:
- After Dr. Gero's death and before Cell reaches Perfect Form.
- Alternatively during another Android-era downtime.

Campaign treatment:
- Preserve Gero-computer android logic.
- Adjust Gohan's forms and Goku's available techniques to campaign state.

## Broly — The Legendary Super Saiyan

Best placement:
- During the ten-day wait before the Cell Games.

Known issues:
- Goku and Gohan are shown in base rather than maintaining Grade 4.
- Goku defeats Broly rather than relying on Gohan's potential.

Campaign treatment:
- Strong fit, but character forms and strategy should match campaign facts.

## Bojack Unbound

Best placement:
- After the Cell Games.

Campaign treatment:
- Very easy to integrate.
- Future Trunks' presence should be explained through timing or a return visit.

## Broly — Second Coming

Best placement:
- During the training period before the 25th World Martial Arts Tournament.

Known issues:
- Goten recognizes Goku despite not having met him in canon.
- Dragon Ball recharge timing may require adjustment.

Campaign treatment:
- Easy to adapt if Broly's first movie occurred.

## Bio-Broly

Possible placement:
- Shortly after the 25th Tournament or during an adapted Buu-era downtime.

Known issues:
- Canon Buu pacing leaves almost no downtime.

Campaign treatment:
- Best moved to after Buu or rewritten as a side operation during a longer divergence.

## Fusion Reborn

Canon compatibility:
- Very poor in its original form.

Campaign treatment:
- Treat as an Otherworld/reality-collapse arc.
- Best adapted after Buu, during a Timeline Fracture, or as an alternate branch.
- Do not force original character placements if they conflict.

## Wrath of the Dragon

Best placement:
- After the Buu Saga.

Campaign treatment:
- Strong fit once Earth is restored and the cast is alive.
- Excellent introduction point for Tapion, Hirudegarn, and Dragon Fist.

## Placement Priority

When inserting a movie:

1. Check required characters and forms.
2. Check Dragon Ball availability.
3. Check current deaths and injuries.
4. Check villain motive against campaign history.
5. Adjust power and techniques to the actual timeline.
6. Rewrite or delay the movie if contradictions remain.
