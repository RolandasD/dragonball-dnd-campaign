# Training & Progression Systems

This file defines the major training philosophies and environmental progression systems used by the campaign.

## Confidence Labels

- **Strong Default**: safe campaign rule.
- **Continuity-Specific / Inferred**: useful but not universal across all media.
- **Speculative**: optional interpretation only.

## Master Roshi — Turtle School

### Philosophy

- Build overwhelming fundamentals before chasing advanced techniques.
- There is always a higher ceiling.
- Strength, speed, stamina, discipline, and practical life experience matter together.

### Methods

- heavy turtle shells
- labor-based conditioning
- milk delivery
- farming and construction work
- endurance training
- practical martial arts drills

### Mechanical Effects

- strong physical-stat growth
- excellent stamina foundation
- improved speed after weight removal
- efficient basic combat habits

## Korin

### Philosophy

- Eliminate wasted motion.
- Control breathing.
- Improve reaction, balance, awareness, and efficiency.

### Mechanical Effects

- movement efficiency
- reflex improvement
- better endurance economy
- deceptive martial skill

## Kami and Mr. Popo

### Philosophy

- Transition from physical brawling toward spiritual awareness.
- Sense energy without relying on eyesight.
- Empty the mind and reduce unnecessary thought.

### Mechanical Effects

- ki sensing
- spiritual awareness
- calm combat state
- movement without hesitation
- early foundation for autonomous reaction concepts

### Clarification

Mr. Popo's teaching can foreshadow Ultra Instinct principles, but it does not automatically teach Ultra Instinct.

## King Kai

### Environment

- approximately 10x Earth gravity

### Philosophy

- adapt the body to extreme force
- manipulate external and multiplied ki
- balance risky output with control

### Techniques

- Kaioken
- Spirit Bomb

### Kaioken

- temporarily multiplies output
- risks muscular tearing, ki instability, and bodily damage
- requires high control and conditioning

### Spirit Bomb

- gathers energy from living beings and the environment
- does not rely solely on the user's internal reserves
- requires time, focus, cooperation, and an opening

## Yardrat Spirit Control

### Philosophy

- perfect equilibrium between body, mind, and spirit
- not primarily physical combat training

### Possible Techniques

- Instant Transmission
- cloning
- gigantification
- Forced Spirit Fission and related advanced applications where appropriate

### Mechanical Effects

- precise ki control
- utility abilities
- improved energy efficiency
- resistance to spiritual imbalance

## Whis / Angel Training

### Philosophy

- eliminate ki leakage
- refine divine ki
- remove the delay caused by conscious processing
- develop autonomous bodily reaction

### Mechanical Effects

- divine ki efficiency
- refined movement
- extreme reaction improvement
- progression toward Ultra Instinct principles

### Limitation

Ultra Instinct is not merely a numeric multiplier. It is a state and technique requiring extraordinary mental and physical mastery.

## Environmental Modifiers

### Gravity Training

- artificially increases physical stress and adaptation demands
- particularly effective for Saiyans and exceptionally durable fighters
- should not be treated as a free Zenkai generator
- excessive gravity causes injury, overtraining, or death

### Hyperbolic Time Chamber / Room of Spirit and Time

Strong default properties:

- one outside day is approximately one inside year
- approximately 10x Earth gravity
- extreme heat and cold
- thin atmosphere
- severe isolation and psychological pressure

Original limitation:

- approximately two subjective years total before the original doorway becomes unusable or the user risks being trapped

Later continuity may modify or bypass this limitation.

## Campaign Training Resolution

Training results depend on:

1. mentor compatibility
2. method quality
3. available time
4. effort and discipline
5. rest and nutrition
6. injuries
7. psychology
8. prior foundation
9. species biology
10. sparring partners and real combat experience

No training method grants guaranteed canon results. Characters may grow differently based on their choices and circumstances.
